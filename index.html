About

Yash_Pndtt is Nitish Pandey, a singer, lyricist, music producer and filmmaker from Delhi. His songs mix Hindi, Bhojpuri and contemporary sounds with stories people recognise from everyday life.

He is the founder of Redoven Media Production House, which produces his music and shoots his videos.
